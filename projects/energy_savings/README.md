So, the idea of the project is to calculate how much energy population of any european country could save if its population will do a weekly recommended by WHO (World Healt Organisation) amount of exercises on energy saving gym machins.

For such purpose we'll need to gather data from Eurostat. Also, we'll need some data that could be found on the internet, like gym machines specifications and recommended norm of exercises by age group from WHO.

1) Recommendations from WHO - https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity#:~:text=Should%20do%20at%20least%2060,least%203%20times%20per%20week.

2) Population in european countries - https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tps00001/default/table?lang=en&category=t_demo.t_demo_pop

3) Population by age groups - https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tps00010/default/table?lang=en&category=t_demo.t_demo_ind

4) Amount of energy, produced on bike - "An average 1-hour workout can produce approximately 160 watt-hours of electricity"
   (https://www.gosportsart.com/eco-powr-questions-answers/#:~:text=An%20average%201%2Dhour%20workout%20can%20produce%20approximately%20160%20watt%2Dhours%20of%20electricity.%20However%2C%20the%20amount%20of%20energy%20can%20vary%20greatly%20based%20on%20the%20intensity%20and%20resistance%20settings%20of%20the%20workout%2C%20up%20to%20a%20maximum%20of%20250%20watt%2Dhours%20per%20workout)
